Brightora

Capstone project, 2026 to 2027. In development.

An AI-powered CRM built as a multi-tenant SaaS. It targets two problems sales teams keep running into: CRM screens that are too complex to use day to day, and customer mood and account health that nobody tracks until a deal is already lost.

  • Each company's data is isolated down to the database, with PostgreSQL row-level security and role-based permissions on every action.
  • AI reads conversations to track customer sentiment and account health, and an AI sales manager explains why an employee's deals went well or badly.
  • An embeddable chat widget lets customers talk to the business from its own website.
  • An MCP interface lets outside AI assistants work inside the CRM, limited to what the key owner's role allows.

Node.js, PostgreSQL, vanilla JavaScript, Claude API, Model Context Protocol

The code and a public demo will be published after the capstone presentation.

Anchors as Live Calibrators

Research paper, Wireless Networks course, 2026

Sensor networks that track a moving target usually report a confidence region, but that region quietly breaks when walls appear or signals drift. My method uses the network's own anchor nodes as live calibrators, so the region stays honest without ever knowing where the target really is.

In 600 simulation runs, when walls appeared or signals drifted, the standard approaches contained the target only 2 to 15% of the time against a 90% goal. This method kept at least 97%, and it flagged a faulty anchor within 4 seconds in every run, with no false alarms.

Python, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, LaTeX, conformal prediction, Kalman filtering
